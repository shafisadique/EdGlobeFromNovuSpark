Our Features

EdGlobe offers a suite of powerful tools designed to streamline school operations, enhance collaboration, and drive efficiency across all aspects of education management.

Academic Suite

Empower educators and students with digital tools that simplify academic workflows, from enrollment to performance tracking, fostering a more organized and effective learning environment.

Smart Admissions

Streamline the enrollment process with online application forms, automated document verification, and real-time status updates. This module reduces paperwork, minimizes errors, and allows administrators to manage inquiries and approvals efficiently, ensuring a smooth onboarding experience for new students and families.

Automated Attendance

Effortlessly track student presence using biometric scanners, mobile check-ins, or RFID technology. Generate instant reports on attendance patterns, send automated alerts to parents for absences, and integrate with other modules to maintain accurate records that support better student engagement and compliance.

Gradebook & Results

Maintain a digital ledger for assignments, exams, and grades with easy input and calculation tools. Produce customizable report cards, analyze performance trends, and securely share results with parents and students, helping to identify areas for improvement and celebrate achievements in real time.

Digital Timetable

Create flexible class schedules that automatically detect conflicts and optimize resource allocation. Teachers and students can access updated timetables via portals or apps, with options for adjustments due to holidays or events, ensuring smooth daily operations and better time management.

Event & Noticeboard

Centralize announcements, event calendars, and important notices in a digital board accessible to all stakeholders. Schedule reminders, attach documents or links, and track views to ensure key information reaches parents, students, and staff promptly, enhancing community involvement and coordination.

People Management

Manage all human aspects of school operations, from student portals to staff administration, promoting collaboration and secure access for everyone involved.

Student & Parent Portal

Provide a secure online space where students and parents can view academic progress, attendance records, fee status, and upcoming events. This portal facilitates direct communication with teachers, submission of assignments, and feedback, strengthening the home-school connection and encouraging active participation in education.

Teacher Workspace

Equip educators with a personalized dashboard for lesson planning, grading, student tracking, and resource sharing. Collaborate on curricula, access teaching materials, and monitor class performance, enabling teachers to focus more on instruction and less on administrative tasks for improved classroom outcomes.

Staff HR & Payroll

Handle employee records, recruitment, performance evaluations, and payroll calculations in a unified system. Automate salary disbursements, track leaves and benefits, and generate compliance reports, ensuring efficient HR operations and supporting staff satisfaction through transparent processes.

ID & Certificate

Design and print personalized ID cards and certificates with embedded photos, barcodes, and security features. Pull data directly from student or staff profiles for accuracy, and batch process for efficiency, simplifying identification and recognition within the school community.

Role-based Access

Define user permissions based on roles such as admin, teacher, or parent to control access to sensitive data. This security feature prevents unauthorized views or edits, logs activities for audits, and maintains data integrity, protecting the school's information ecosystem.

Finance & Operations

Optimize financial management and operational tasks with tools that ensure accuracy, transparency, and scalability for growing institutions.

Online Fee Collection

Allow parents to pay tuition and other fees securely online with instant confirmations and digital receipts. Set up recurring payments, send due reminders, and reconcile collections automatically, reducing late payments and administrative overhead for financial stability.

Accounting Dashboard

View comprehensive financial overviews with customizable dashboards displaying budgets, expenses, revenues, and forecasts. Drill down into transactions, generate balance sheets, and monitor cash flow in real time, empowering informed decision-making for school leaders.

Payment Gateways

Integrate popular payment processors to accept diverse methods like cards, digital wallets, and bank transfers. Ensure secure transactions with encryption and compliance standards, facilitating quick and convenient payments while minimizing processing fees and errors.

Bulk Data Import

Upload large volumes of data such as student lists, financial records, or inventory details from spreadsheets or databases. Validate imports for accuracy, map fields automatically, and save hours on manual entry, ideal for migrations or annual updates.

Custom Reports

Build and export tailored reports on metrics like enrollment trends, financial summaries, or performance analytics. Use filters, charts, and templates for easy customization, supporting regulatory compliance and strategic planning with data-driven insights.

Data Backup & Security

Schedule automatic backups to cloud or local storage, with encryption and recovery options to safeguard against data loss. Implement multi-factor authentication and regular audits to protect sensitive information, ensuring reliability and peace of mind for all users.

Integrations

Seamlessly connect EdGlobe with external services to extend functionality, improve communication, and ensure compliance in a connected ecosystem.

WhatsApp & SMS Alerts

Deliver instant notifications for urgent updates, such as attendance issues or event reminders, via WhatsApp and SMS. Customize message templates, track delivery, and integrate with other modules for automated alerts, boosting engagement and timely responses from parents and staff.

Live

Enable live classes and virtual sessions through integrated video platforms, supporting remote learning and hybrid models. Schedule sessions, record for later access, and track participation, ensuring continuity in education during disruptions while enhancing interactive teaching.

DLT Compliance

Adhere to regulatory requirements for messaging services, such as India's DLT platform for SMS, to prevent spam and ensure legal communications. Register templates, monitor compliance, and integrate seamlessly, maintaining trust and avoiding penalties in regulated environments.

Third Party APIs

Link with external applications like CRM systems, learning management tools, or analytics platforms via APIs. Customize integrations for data syncing and expanded features, allowing schools to build a tailored ecosystem that grows with their needs.

Digital Marketing

Leverage tools for email campaigns, social media posts, and targeted ads to promote school programs and events. Track engagement metrics, segment audiences, and automate outreach, helping to attract new enrollments and strengthen community relations effectively.
